Root Cause Analysis:
In at-least-once message queues, worker crashes or network timeouts after a database
commit prevent the acknowledgment (`ACK`) from reaching Redis, prompting the broker
to re-deliver the task.
When `click_event_id` and the `ON CONFLICT (click_event_id) DO NOTHING` clause are omitted
from the SQL insert, the worker treats every delivery as an independent new event.
Replayed or retried tasks insert redundant rows into `click_events`, corrupting analytical
counters (`GET /links/{id}/analytics`) and double-counting client clicks.
